February Housing Market Update

Buyer confidence continued to build in February, with stronger attendance at open homes and a noticeable lift in sales activity across Auckland. Lower price points remained especially active, while well-presented homes in desirable locations attracted solid competition.

Published: 1 February 2026 Category: Housing Market Update

Overview

February saw a steady increase in buyer enquiry, particularly in the under-$1,000,000 bracket. Vendors who priced realistically and presented their homes well were rewarded with stronger turnout and more confident offers.

First-home buyers remained an important part of the market, helped by softer prices in some suburbs. Investors were also more active than in previous months, particularly where rental demand remained strong.

What we noticed this month

  • Open home attendance improved across many suburbs.
  • Affordable homes continued to attract the strongest interest.
  • Well-marketed listings stood out in a competitive environment.
  • Buyers remained careful, but were more willing to act than late last year.

Looking ahead

If current confidence continues, we would expect the market to remain active through early autumn. Sellers should focus on pricing, presentation, and strong photography, while buyers should be prepared to move quickly on quality listings.

“The market is still price-sensitive, but the number of active buyers is clearly improving.”
